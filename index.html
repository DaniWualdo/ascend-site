Growing up, I was always the chubby kid. I went through a lot of bullying, and by 16 I weighed 240 pounds. I'd look in the mirror and feel I was too young to feel that bad.

Read the full storyShow less

I wanted to change, but I didn't know how. I barely ate once a day and just ran and ran. In six months I dropped to 160 pounds, while wearing myself down physically and mentally. It was a really low point: I talked to myself cruelly and kept pushing, even though I was hurting myself.

In 2023 I started going to the gym and, little by little, built a healthier relationship with myself. I developed discipline, met incredible people and learned to take care of myself and love myself.

That's where ASCEND comes from. I know what it's like to want to change and feel lost. I want to help you progress, listen when something gets hard and give you genuine support. I want you to feel confident talking to me and know we're in this together.

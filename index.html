Your coach

From the chubby kid to your gym partner.

1:1Every client
2023Training since
22Years old

Growing up, I was always the chubby kid. I got bullied for my weight for most of my childhood, and it took a toll on my confidence. During the pandemic I got up to 240 pounds, and at 17 I decided something had to change. In 2023 I started training and found out the gym was about way more than looking good. It taught me discipline, consistency and, above all, to trust myself.

Today I'm 22 and at the best point of my life, physically and mentally. That's why I started Ascend. I know what it feels like to not be comfortable with yourself and to want to change without knowing where to start. I was there too.

I'm not a trainer with a clipboard watching you suffer. I train next to you, check in when you skip and celebrate every PR with you. You don't have to be the strongest or have any experience to start. We all start somewhere, and I'm here to walk that road with you.
